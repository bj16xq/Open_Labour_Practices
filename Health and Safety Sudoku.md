# Content

While traditional sudoku uses numbers 1-9, the game can be themed to replace numbers with different values.\
\
For example, "[periodic table sudoku](https://scilearn.sydney.edu.au/fychemistry/games/su_doku.shtml)" exists.\
\
To integrate health and safety concepts into a sudoku puzzle, the number values can be replaces with health and safety concepts.
For this to work, each concept must be mutually exclusive.
To have 9 distinct values, two overall H&S concepts will be integrated. The 1st is RACE (recognize, assess, control, evaluate) which has 4 distinct components. The 2nd is the 5 forms of hazard control: elimination, substitution, engineering, administrative, and PPE (personal protective equipment). Together, these two concepts adds up to 9 distinct values. Thus, in place of numbers, H&S sudoku can have the following values:\
1 = recognize\
2 = assess\
3 = control\
4 = evaluate\
5 = elimination\
6 = substitution\
7 = engineering\
8 = administrative\
9 = PPE
